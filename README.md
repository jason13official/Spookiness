# Spookiness, for NeoForge 26.1.2

A Halloween/Autumn themed mod created for [SpookyJam 2026](https://spooky-jam.com/)! Haunted blocks that come alive, the Pumpkin Mace trials, pumpkin allies, three harvest bosses, and spooky poltergeist activity!

## Content

### Items and Blocks

- **Pieces of Lament**: craftable or dropped by the Wickman/Frostwick and Hallowed Mother; combine into the Lament Configuration.
- **Lament Configuration**: use it for a fiery ritual that carries you to the Nether's closest Netherrealm arena; and then back to where you used it in the Overworld.
- **Soulless Jack o'Mimic**: the arena altar; place the Lament Configuration in it to awaken a boss (Gourdwyrm), take it back out to forfeit.
- **Harvest Crown**: a thin gold crown with a tiny jack o'lantern for the forepiece; gives night vision, lights your way, and Jack o'Mimics won't target you.
- **Gourdwyrm Trophy**: a decorative trophy block. (please like this please)

### Mobs

- **Jack o'Mimic**: a hopping, biting pumpkin that spawns naturally in Pumpkin Patch friendly biomes; may drop Jack o'Mimic Seeds when killed by a player.
- **Lurking Jack o'Mimic**: looks like a vanilla carved pumpkin or jack o'lantern; wakes into a Jack o'Mimic after a player lingers nearby for 5-8 seconds or when hit/right-clicked!
- **Floating Candles**: candles near you can wake while you hold the Pumpkin Mace or during the Harvest (Haunted Harvest Night); touch them to start a conga line of up to 12, and a 13th turns the line into 3 Spectral Jack o'Mimic allies!
- **Floating Book**: wakes from an enchanting table when you enchant (enemy), or from nearby chiseled bookshelves (neutral); returns to its home shelf like a bee, and can drop lapis.
- **Floating Lantern**: hanging soul lanterns wake at night; right-click to claim one (one per player); it lights your way and sears nearby hostiles; sneak-right-click to release it.
- **Floating Skull**: skeleton skulls near you wake at night; skeletons killed at night can leave one behind.
- **Floating Sword**: can rise from a campfire when food finishes cooking; hostile.
- **Floating Shears**: can appear when shearing sheep or when a lamb grows up; shears sheep and leaves.
- **Floating Hoe**: can appear when tilling or when a crop fully grows; tills soil.
- **Haunted Armor Stand**: armor stands near you get haunted at night; they turn and hop toward you when you aren't looking, and revert at dawn.
- **Spectral Jack o'Mimic**: a ghostly flying companion that fights for you.

### Bosses

- **The Wickman**: kindle (right-click) a jack-o-lantern headed mob with the Pumpkin Mace to summon it; spawns healing Vigil Candles nearby, throws pumpkin bombs, and its head breaks off to fight on its own at low health; it's weaker in the rain and drops Piece of Lament, One.
- **The Frostwick**: kindle (right-click) a snow golem instead for a frost variant of the Wickman that throws frost volleys. (also drops Piece of Lament, One)
- **Hallowed Mother**: rises from a pumpkin-patch field at night once you have 5 hallowed allies (accept or deny the prompt in chat); spits out Jack o'Mimic broods, drags your allies with a vine, eats, and leaves poison clouds nearby. She retreats at dawn, and drops Piece of Lament, Two.
- **Gourdwyrm**: a segmented pumpkin wyrm in the Netherrealm arena (in the Nether); circles, burrows, and charges with different attack phases; drops the Harvest Crown and a Gourdwyrm Trophy.

## Progression / Mechanics

### Pumpkin Mace

- **Pumpkin Mace**: crafted from a pumpkin and a stick; smash attacks like the vanilla mace, but from a shorter initial distance.
- **Mace Growth**: kills of pumpkin-headed mobs evolve it: Carved (5), Lit (15, gives light), Blazing (25, brighter and ignites targets), Thorned (50, +5 thorn damage).
- **Spectral Companions**: every 5 pumpkin kills with the mace summons a Spectral Jack o'Mimic ally.
- **Pumpkin King**: lantern-headed mobs won't target a player holding the Pumpkin Mace.

### Allies

- **Hallowing**: right-click a carved-pumpkin headed mob with the Pumpkin Mace to make it your ally; costs 1 heart and 15 durability, up to 5 allies.
- **Hallowed Allies**: follow and defend you, hunt nearby monsters, hit harder at night, teleport to you when far away, and are logged out with you when you log out.
- **Stay**: right-click your hallowed ally (empty hand; any item for Jack o'Mimics) to make it stay or follow, like a tamed wolf.
- **Jack o'Mimic Seeds**: plant on farmland; the stem remembers who planted it and grows dormant pumpkins that wake after 30 seconds as that player's allied Jack o'Mimic (max 10 per player per dimension).

### Haunted Harvest Mechanics

- **Haunted Harvest Night**: every 8th night in the Overworld (1st night and onward); candles, books, shears, and more awaken more often!
- **Pumpkin Patches**: can generate in plains and forests, with stems, vines, grass, an active Jack o'Mimic, and an enemy lurking in the open...
- **Pumpkin Heads**: zombies, skeletons and their variants sometimes spawn wearing a carved pumpkin (15%), sometimes lit as a Jack o'Lantern.
- **Pumpkin Smashing**: hitting a pumpkin, carved pumpkin or jack o'lantern with a Mace or Pumpkin Mace smashes it instantly with normal drops; 10% are rotten and burst into a poison cloud.
- **Dynamic Light**: lit mobs, jack o'lantern heads and lit maces light up the area around them.
- **Advancements**: a full *Spookiness* advancement tree.
